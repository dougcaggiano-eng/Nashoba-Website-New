How I work

A game plan before every job

I walk the property and map out the job before I start. That way you know exactly what you're getting, and nothing gets missed.

  1. 01

    Free in-person estimate

    I come out, look at everything, and give you an accurate price. Send a photo first and I can give you a range.

  2. 02

    We agree on the details

    Which surfaces, which areas, anything you're unsure about. Clear up front, so there are no surprises after.

  3. 03

    Rinse, treat, protect

    Surfaces get pre-rinsed before any cleaner goes on. Plants and windows are rinsed down to protect them from the wash.

  4. 04

    Bottom up, top down

    On houses I apply from the ground up so it won't streak, then rinse from the top down and check every runoff spot.

Careful where it counts

  • Wood gets very low pressure and no bleach. A little too much pressure can hurt it.
  • Windows and fabrics are kept clear and rinsed to prevent stains.
  • Working near a pool? I'll let you know beforehand that a little dirt may get in.
  • If a surface is already damaged (oxidized or broken siding, a chewed-up wood deck), I'll tell you honestly rather than make it worse.

Questions

Good to know

Are you taking new customers?

Yes. I'm booking estimates now for the upcoming season. Send a request below with what you need and I'll get back to you quickly.

How much does it cost?

Every job is priced individually based on the size, the surfaces, and how much work it takes. The free in-person estimate gives you an exact price. If you send a photo first, I can give you a ballpark range.

What do you need from me on the day?

An outdoor water source I can hook up to. I work on dry days. A light sprinkle is fine, but I won't wash in the rain.

Is there anything you won't clean?

If cleaning could cause more damage, I'll say so. That includes badly worn or chewed-up wood decks, heavily oxidized siding, broken siding, and old siding that hasn't been painted in a long time.

How do I pay?

Cash, check, or Venmo, paid directly to me.

Why does power washing matter beyond looks?

Mold and grime don't just look bad. Left alone, they break surfaces down over time, which costs more to fix later. Regular cleaning protects what you have.
