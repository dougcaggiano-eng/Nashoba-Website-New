## 8. Equipment, materials and method
(I use a Honda GX200 power washer. I then have a foam cannon I use to apply my bleach on the house. I will use a pump up sprayer for smaller places where I need to be more percise about my bleach usage. Bleach is used to kill mold and remove grime from surfaces. I use it it almost all of my jobs. I have to be very careful with it in some instances because it can damage anything with a fabric. I keep bleach away from wood, I try to keep it away from windows because it can leave annoying stains. I make sure to rinse down any surface with water before applying bleach. If I apply bleach first, then it may react with the surface in a negative way leaving perminant marks. If I am working around a pool I tell the customer that some dirt may get in the pool. For large surfaces I use a rounded surface cleaner. I use a turbo nozzel for fences which works like a charm. I am extra careful around wood because a little pressure will hurt it. 
Whenever using bleach, I make sure to thouroughly rinse down every surface to protect plants and other surfaces from the bleach. I rinse windows down to protect from that ugly stain. The way I wash houses is apply the bleach from the ground up so it doesn't leave uneven streaks, and rinse from the top down, spraying almost paralell  to the siding to almost kick off the bleach. I make sure to see any drop off points that water runs off to ensure that bleach doesn't build up in those areas.)
